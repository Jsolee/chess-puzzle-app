# Scachs design system

Tokens: `brand/tokens.json`. Implementation: `Scachs/Design/Theme.swift`. Brand rules: `brand/BRAND.md`.

- **Colour strategy:** committed. The verd field `#0f3a33` carries every screen; gold `#e7b96c` is the one accent for interactive things.
- **The field is lamplit** (`FieldBackground`, `.fieldBackground()`): an ellipse of green light falls from the top centre and is gone a third of the way down, the bottom deepens to `#092722`, and a fine grain at 9 % keeps it from looking printed. No pattern on it.
- **Scene:** someone on a sofa at 11 pm with ten minutes, holding the phone low in lamplight. That forces a deep, low-luminance field with bright objects on it.
- **Type:** `Theme.display` (Grenze Gotisch, 28 pt and up, titles only). `Theme.text` (Ysabeau Office) for everything else, with a 1.25 step between levels. Screen titles are big and left-aligned.
- **Gold wire under titles:** `ScreenTitle` and `SectionTitle` put a lozenge and a fading gold wire under the name; a section's is shorter and fainter. Above a board, `BoardHeader` uses a gold rubric in tracked capitals ("Avui · dissabte, 14 de novembre") instead of a big screen title, so the board comes up.
- **Layout:** the board is full-bleed edge to edge. Content sits on the field with hairlines and space; no cards, no nested surfaces. Rows, not tiles. A game row leads with its final position as a miniature and names the opening in the app's language (`OpeningNames`).
- **Elevation:** none on the field. Only pieces cast shadows.
- **Motion:** 150-250 ms, ease-out quart, no bounce. The reflex wave is the single signature. `Reduce Motion` freezes tilt and shrinks the wave to a flash.
- **Glass:** system tab bar and toolbars only (iOS 26+ gives it free).
- **Tab icons** are our own, filled like the pieces: a sun, the knight from `brand/pieces/silhouettes.py`, a pennant, a wax seal, a shield with a lozenge (`Assets.xcassets/tab.*`, template SVG). Rows on Avui carry the same marks in a gold-ringed `Medallion`.
- **Components:** primary action is tin text with weight on the field, or gold for the current one. Buttons have no fill unless they're the screen's single primary action, then a tin cartouche (`.buttonStyle(.cartouche)`): corners cut in, a bronze wire inside the edge, ink text; gold only for an answer that was right. Controls are wire: `WireToggleStyle` (a hollow bead off, a tin bead in a gold track on), `SectionPicker` (the chosen name in tin over a gold wire and lozenge), `BeadProgress` for a lesson's steps. `CloseButton` is the one way out of a full-screen page. Hit areas 44 pt minimum.
- **Move-class badge:** a small enamel disc with a dark glyph; `MoveClassBadge`. Classes are told apart by glyph, then colour.
- **Numbers:** always Ysabeau Office, tabular by default. Accuracy and rating read like instruments: the eval graph sits in a gold bezel (`.wireFrame()`) with its line in gold, and a review's classes are bars facing each other, empty ones hidden until asked.
- **Heraldry and wax:** the profile's crest is an enamelled shield in a gold wire, like the pieces, tinctures by the rule of metal on colour, shown large and centred as the head of the page; settings live in their own sheet behind the gear. A finished lesson is a red wax seal with its Roman numeral; numerals are set in Ysabeau Office, since blackletter Roman numerals don't read. The lesson path joins the seals with a gold thread as far as you've come, dots beyond.
- **The Atlas:** the one exception to the field: a vellum portolan sheet, full-bleed like the board, painted per pixel (`AtlasPainter`). Its washes are pigments, not accents: green where you do well, ochre where it's even, vermilion where it costs you.
- **La carta** (`Scachs/Puzzles/Chart/`): the second sheet of vellum, sister to the Atlas. Every puzzle theme is a land in one of five realms; a land is fog until three puzzles chart it, and gets a gold flag once a dozen blind puzzles there go better than your rating expects. Flags never come down (`chartHeld`), and navigator ranks climb with the flags held. The world grows from fixed seeds, so everyone sails the same chart. The beasts (dragon, sea serpent, kraken, the port's ship) are enamel in gold wire like the pieces, each in its own small canvas so only they redraw; `Reduce Motion` stills them.
